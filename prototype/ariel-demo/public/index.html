Four deliberately separate steps

  1. 01InterpretThe model drafts concise prose.
  2. 02SelectIt chooses one allowed reference token.
  3. 03ReconstructThe server slices immutable UTF-8 bytes.
  4. 04VerifyClaim Gate checks identity, hash, range, and exact text.
What “verified” means here

The quotation is deterministically verified. The interpretation is model-generated and is not semantically verified. Exact source identity, integrity, range, and quotation are checked against the pinned local corpus. Semantic entailment and source authority are not: Sefaria supplies text and edition metadata, while SHA-256 detects byte changes.
